# Privacy Policy for AIWiki

**Last Updated: March 3, 2026**

### Introduction
Welcome to AIWiki ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the AIWiki iOS application (the "App").

### Data Collection and Usage
**We do not collect any personal data.**

AIWiki is designed as a completely offline application:
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information to use the App.
* **No Network Requests:** The App does not make any background network requests to our servers. All AI tool data is pre-bundled within the application.
* **Local Storage Only:** Any data you generate while using the App, such as your search history or your list of "Favorite" tools, is stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising trackers. We do not monitor how you use the App.

### Data Sharing
Because we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or disclose any of your data to third parties.

### Children's Privacy
The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
