### App name

`AIWiki`

### Subtitle

`离线 AI 工具百科与提示词助手`

### Promotional text

`离线浏览热门 AI 工具、对比优劣、收藏常用产品，并用提示词工作室快速整理高质量 Prompt。`

### Keywords

`AI工具,提示词,ChatGPT,Claude,Gemini,AI搜索,效率,写作,编程,图像生成`

### Description

```text
AIWiki 是一款离线可用的 AI 工具百科应用，帮你更快找到合适的 AI 产品、理解差异，并沉淀自己的使用方法。

你可以用 AIWiki 做这些事：
- 浏览整理后的 AI 工具资料与学习材料
- 按分类查找写作、编程、搜索、图像、视频等不同方向的工具
- 对比两个工具的特点、优势、限制与适用场景
- 收藏常用工具，记录个人笔记和使用偏好
- 使用提示词工作室快速生成更清晰的 Prompt
- 通过仪表盘查看自己的 AI 使用轨迹与能力分布

为什么适合日常使用：
- 完全离线：无需注册，无需登录
- 本地存储：收藏、笔记、搜索历史保存在设备本地
- 资料结构化：更适合快速查询和横向对比
- 支持国际化：核心界面和内容支持中英文体验

适合人群：
- 想系统了解 AI 工具的新用户
- 想快速筛选工具的内容创作者、产品经理、开发者
- 想沉淀提示词和工作流的重度 AI 用户

AIWiki 不提供模型调用服务，也不在 App 内出售第三方 AI 账号。部分工具详情页可跳转到对应官方公开网站，方便你进一步查看原始资料。
```

## `en-US`

### App name

`AIWiki`

### Subtitle

`Offline AI tools guide and prompt studio`

### Promotional text

`Browse AI tools offline, compare options quickly, save favorites, and draft better prompts with a built-in prompt studio.`

### Keywords

`AI tools,prompts,ChatGPT,Claude,Gemini,search,productivity,writing,coding,image generation`

### Description

```text
AIWiki is an offline guide to AI tools, prompt workflows, and practical usage notes. It helps users discover the right tools faster, understand tradeoffs, and keep a personal reference library on-device.

With AIWiki, you can:
- explore curated AI tool profiles and learning materials
- browse categories such as writing, coding, search, image generation, video, and analysis
- compare two tools side by side across features, strengths, limitations, and fit
- save favorites and keep personal notes locally on your device
- generate clearer prompts with the built-in Prompt Studio
- review your usage patterns through a lightweight dashboard

Why people use AIWiki:
- offline first: no account, no sign-in, no required backend
- local storage only: favorites, notes, and history stay on device
- structured references: designed for fast lookup and side-by-side comparison
- localized experience: core UI and content are available in Chinese and English

AIWiki does not provide model inference or sell third-party AI subscriptions inside the app. Some tool detail pages can open the official public websites of those products when the user chooses to continue.
```
